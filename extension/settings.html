Browser translation

Translate locally in Chrome. No account or API key.

  1. Open Hulu or Disney+ with English subtitles.
  2. Choose your language in the extension.
  3. Click Start subtitles on the video if prompted.
Language packs & availability

Chrome prepares the language pack on first use. Subtitle text stays on this computer. Requires a supported desktop Chrome and language. Slang may translate literally. If your browser or organization disables this feature, choose another translator.
